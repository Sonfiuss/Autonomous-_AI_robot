## Decisions
- Primitive `MOVE dx dy` replaces the roadmap's `ARC` (roadmap L4 §2.1): the omni chassis drives a leg
  directly. Default is the non-holonomic ROTATE/FORWARD set; `CM_MV_HOLONOMIC=1` switches.
- MV plans the robot as a disc of `robot.radius` (0.225 m), while `candidates.py` collision-tests the
  real footprint polygon. MV is therefore the stricter of the two. Measured over 40 seeds / 1452 spots:
  95% plan, 4% MV_ERR_NO_PATH (region genuinely sealed for a 0.45 m robot), 0.3% MV_ERR_GOAL_BLOCKED
  (disc does not fit where the polygon does). Every one is reported with a reason, never a path through
  an obstacle. Reconciling the two is an open item in `agent/plan/mv_plan.md`.
- The ctypes layer keeps the library handle as a module-level singleton rather than a class, so callers
  do not each need an instance (standards-review deferral, recorded in the task log).
- 64-bit toolchain required on this PC: `CXX=/c/msys64/mingw64/bin/g++` matches the 64-bit Python at
  `D:\University\Python\Python310\python.exe`. The 32-bit MinGW.org g++ used by the RM tests cannot
  produce a DLL this interpreter can load.
